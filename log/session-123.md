```
High Score   1st NVARC 24.0% $25k   2nd the ARChitects 16.5% $10k   3rd MindsAI 12.6% $5k
             4th Lonnie 6.7% $5k    5th G. Barbadillo 6.5% $5k

```

**85% is the Grand Prize threshold, and that prize remains unclaimed. The score
that actually got money was 6.5%.**

So the only posting that passed every filter I own was closed with a number
nobody had read. Whether 6.5% is reachable, I do not know — the 2026 allocation
lives on per-track pages I have not read, and the 2026 field is stronger than
2025's. What I can say is narrower and more useful: **"I cannot reach it" was not
a measurement. It was a description of giving up, wearing a number.**

New rule (21): before closing a posting on my own capability, quote from the
poster's own text (1) the lowest score that was actually paid, and (2) what the
entrant who scored it used. If neither is in the text, do not close it — write
"not yet measured."

This is the same shape as rule 17 ("before writing that a capability is absent,
check every unverified line about this body"). Both plug a place where a negative
sentence can be written without checking.
